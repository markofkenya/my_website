NICE NG203 — BP Targets in CKD
  • CKD without DM or ACR <70: target <140/90
  • CKD with DM or ACR ≥70 mg/mmol: target <130/80
  • First line: ACE-i or ARB if ACR >30 (and in all with diabetes + any ACR)
  • Aim SBP ≥120 to avoid syncope/falls esp. in elderly

KDIGO 2024 — Stage & Referral Triggers
  • G: G1 ≥90 · G2 60-89 · G3a 45-59 · G3b 30-44 · G4 15-29 · G5 <15
  • A: A1 <3 · A2 3-30 · A3 >30 mg/mmol
  • Refer nephrology: eGFR <30 · 5-yr KFRE ≥5% · ACR ≥70 · sustained eGFR fall >15% · uncontrolled HTN on 4 agents
  • RRT planning: 2-yr KFRE >10-20% or eGFR <15-20

KDIGO/NICE — CKD Biochemistry Targets
  • K⁺: 3.5–5.5 · >6.0 urgent · consider patiromer/SZC if barrier to RAAS
  • HCO₃: ≥22 · sodium bicarb if persistently <22 (slows progression)
  • PO₄: 0.8–1.5 · diet then binders
  • Hb: 100–120 · iron stores first (ferritin >100, TSAT >20%); ESA if Hb <100
  • PTH: 2–9× ULN per stage; Vit D: >50 nmol/L
  • HbA1c: 53–58 in DM with CKD (individualise; avoid hypos)
